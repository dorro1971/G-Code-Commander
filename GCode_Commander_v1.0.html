About GCode Commander v1.0

Professional post-processing for Cura G-code, designed for Bambu printers.

Created by Ian Dorrington to solve practical printing challenges encountered with lightweight aircraft, functional models and specialist G-code.

Design Philosophy

Modify only what you choose. Everything else remains exactly as sliced.
  • Runs entirely in your browser
  • Your G-code never leaves your computer
  • No cloud services
  • No unnecessary modifications

Features

  • Relative extrusion conversion
  • Print & travel speed control
  • Print & travel acceleration editing
  • Extrusion flow adjustment
  • Temperature overrides
  • Automatic purge sequence
  • Recipe save/load
  • Safe end G-code completion
